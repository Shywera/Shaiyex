The long version

How it actually went.

I started in Argus, then quit, because I spent my sub money on my ex. Came back in BFA purely for a transmog — I wanted the Battle of Dazar'alor plate set, so I joined a guild to get it. I stayed through Eternal Palace, at which point it became clear I'd accidentally started raiding and quite liked it.

Then Ny'alotha: an invite to a top-700 guild, 12/12 mythic, and Rank 1 holy paladin for a month straight. The guild disbanded. Shadowlands was six guilds and four classes, most of them ending the same way — one died at 2/10 because it couldn't find a priest, which remains the funniest sentence in this document. I held still through Sanctum and Sepulcher as a warrior, moved up when progress stalled, and when no warrior spot was worth taking, went back to healing for Fated and the start of Dragonflight.

The guild after that was home for a year and a half, until it ended in April 2024 and I stopped for a while. I came back for Queen Ansurek, then a JOB happened, because WORKING IS FUN. Tried again in Dimensius, trialled as a healer somewhere new, and ended it myself when the ask turned into 200 dungeons to gear for two reclears in a season that was already over.

The pattern isn't me leaving good guilds. It's good guilds running out of road. I've never left one that was working, and I'd very much like the next one to make that sentence boring.

Lemon squares.

You've read this far, so you get the recipe. It is called lemon squares and it is, on close inspection, a two-layer cake. I have chosen not to resolve this. It works.

  • Cake
  • 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup softened unsalted butter
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Zest of 2 lemons
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • Lemon glaze
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2–3 tbsp lemon juice
  • Lemon buttercream
  • 1 cup softened unsalted butter
  • 4 cups powdered sugar
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Yellow food colouring (optional)
  1. Preheat oven to 175 °C. Grease and flour two round cake pans.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder and salt together in a bowl. Set aside.
  3. Cream butter and sugar in a large bowl until fluffy.
  4. Beat in eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla.
  5. Add lemon zest and juice to the butter mixture. Mix well.
  6. Gradually add the dry ingredients, alternating with the milk. Start and end with dry. Mix until just combined.
  7. Divide the batter between the pans and smooth the tops.
  8. Bake 25–30 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  9. Cool 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
  10. Whisk powdered sugar and lemon juice for the glaze, adjusting consistency as needed.
  11. Brush the glaze over the cooled cakes to soak up the tangy goodness.
  12. For the buttercream, beat butter until creamy, then gradually add powdered sugar, zest and juice. Add colouring if you want it.
  13. Place one layer on a plate, spread with buttercream, add the second layer, then frost the whole thing.
  14. Decorate with extra zest or lemon slices if you're feeling fancy.
  15. Slice, serve, enjoy.
