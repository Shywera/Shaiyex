The Quiet Part

Not linked from anywhere on the site. There is no way to arrive here by accident.

The front of this site has two faces and both of them are performances. One is polite about the group and grateful for the invite. The other decides who lives. Neither is the whole thing, which is roughly how it goes for everybody who has ever written an About page.

So this is the version with nothing to sell. I heal because being the reason five people got through something is a better feeling than any number on a meter. I get irritated when somebody stands in the obvious thing four times, and then I remember I have done exactly that on a class I did not know. Most bad players are just people who have not learned the fight yet. That is the entire idea behind the trainers on this site.

The rest of it, the chains and the shrine and the upside down cross, is because building something is more fun when it is allowed to be strange.
